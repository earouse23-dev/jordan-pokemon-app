# Mica Step 2 competitor source ledger

Research checked: 2026-08-31

This ledger records the public evidence used by the Step 2 benchmark. It is a
software-requirements input, not a market, positioning, pricing, or business
analysis. No paid account was purchased and no competitor account was changed.

## Evidence rules

| Class                        | Meaning                                                                                                      | Permitted conclusion                                                                                                                                                        |
| ---------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| A — documented workflow      | A current first-party help page or public product walkthrough gives a usable sequence of actions.            | The documented path, fields, and correction behavior may be treated as verified public procedure. Runtime speed and reliability remain unverified unless directly measured. |
| B — current product evidence | A current official app-store listing, release note, or live public product surface describes the capability. | The feature may be treated as currently offered, but exact clicks, defaults, speed, and reliability remain unverified.                                                      |
| C — vendor claim             | A marketing or pricing page claims a capability or outcome without an inspectable workflow.                  | Record only as an unverified claim. Do not make it a requirement by itself.                                                                                                 |
| D — anecdotal evidence       | A current app-store review reports a success or failure.                                                     | Use only as a research lead or risk signal, never as a measured fact.                                                                                                       |
| U — unavailable              | The workflow requires a paid account, sign-in, unsupported device, or unavailable environment.               | Mark it unverified; do not invent a result or buy access.                                                                                                                   |

“Verified” in the benchmark means verified from the cited public evidence, not a
signed-in device test. Mica's acceptance targets are future test thresholds and
must not be read as current performance claims.

## Constraints and gaps

- No paid competitor account was used. Paid-only workflows are class U unless a
  public first-party procedure documents them.
- No iPhone or Android physical-device session was available. Store descriptions
  are not scanner-speed measurements.
- Accessibility declarations were available for some iOS listings but were not
  independently tested with VoiceOver, TalkBack, Switch Control, 200% text, or
  external keyboards.
- Offline behavior was not consistently documented by the named products. The
  benchmark records it as unverified instead of treating silence as “no.”
- “CardGrade” is interpreted as CardGrade.io, matching the named product. It is
  not CardGrader.AI.
- User reviews identify risks but do not establish error rates, causes, or current
  reproducibility.
