### R1 — Valuation provenance

- `lib/portfolio.js` rejects a graded copy's generic `price` even when `pricingStatus` is `live`. A ready `mica-exact-sold-v1` result in the same native currency is required for current graded value and comparable P/L. All graded historical valuation observations are excluded until a durable, context-validated exact-sold read path exists; existing generic market rows remain reference history only. Raw and sealed selected live references continue through their existing path.
- `app.js` derives the graded result from the existing detail sales cache and `exactSoldValuation`, whose verified envelope checks canonical printing, language, finish, edition, promo, grade, label, currency, completed sales and freshness. Failed, stale, insufficient and unresolved results do not become portfolio value. The detail keeps its separate reference-market display. Collection gain/loss and P/L filters use the eligible price; movement filters cannot turn a graded generic index series into sold-derived movement.
- `capturePortfolioValuation` saves only the raw/sealed subset to the existing aggregate snapshot RPC. Graded units are counted as unpriced there. A ready in-memory graded estimate **is deliberately not written** to this provenance-free aggregate; no provider is contacted automatically.
- The new browser production-path case injects a synthetic fresh €900 graded index: **0 priced units and 0 snapshot writes**. A validated three-sale exact context yields **€120 current value and €70 P/L** in memory. Changing its grade or making sales stale returns to unpriced. The existing late-sale selection guard was retained and passed in the affected physical-copy suite. After fresh database login, a synthetic graded €900 generic observation remains readable as reference history but does not contribute to value or P/L.

**Durability boundary:** Current exact-sale evidence lives only in the selected detail's owner/session-guarded memory. The existing `position_price_observations` table is service-write-only; the bounded owner history RPC does not return a validated exact-sold context/evidence contract, and the aggregate snapshot RPC stores amount/coverage without per-position source lineage. `/api/sales` is a read-only GET and does not authenticate an owner or persist a result. The present schema/path cannot prove a graded sold estimate after reload. No fixture-only cache is described as durable proof. The smallest additive follow-up is an authenticated server-side write of the _server-validated_ exact-sold result and its canonical context, contributing sale IDs/dates, as-of/freshness, rule version and owner/position ID, plus an owner-scoped read projection and provenance-linked aggregate snapshot rule. That requires a reviewed additive database/API change; none was authored or applied here. Until then graded historical sold-derived lines and graded snapshots remain blocked.

### R2 — Events, membership and costs

`portfolioProfitLossHistory` now includes actual dated purchases, sales and known lot acquisitions in addition to observations. The selected day's price is still required for active value; event-only dates never reuse a prior or current quote. Sale-only dates retain realized P/L, and a fully sold known position has a known zero active value. Undated acquisition lots are reported as unknown historical membership instead of disappearing from coverage. The calculator and production row hydration validate numeric lot totals, remaining cost, sale proceeds and allocated sale basis even when known flags are optimistic. It keeps true zero distinct, reconciles a partial sale against multiple lots, and excludes incompatible sale currency. The original A/B/C table above continues to pass unchanged in the unit reconciliation and the disposable database proof.

### R3 — Native currency and chart coverage

The existing history panel now selects USD or EUR when both occur, and automatically uses EUR for an EUR-only collection. P/L and Value use the chosen native currency's dated observations, ledger and coverage. Mode, range and collection filters survive currency switching. Neither historical FX nor transaction conversion is used. The retained Price change view is explicitly labeled **Reference price change** and remains separate from sold-derived portfolio P/L.

Value and P/L show complete dates with connected round points and partial known amounts as unconnected diamonds. Missing price, unknown basis, unknown membership date and unknown sale basis/proceeds appear in visible coverage, accessible date summaries and tooltips. A held unpriced copy is never a known zero; a fully liquidated known portfolio can be. Both modes expose concise empty/loading/error/retry states. The chart does not connect through an unknown date or treat a change in the priced subset as complete investment return.
