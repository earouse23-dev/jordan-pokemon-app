## Source decision and observed format

Use the [ECB euro foreign exchange reference rates](https://www.ecb.europa.eu/stats/policy_and_exchange_rates/euro_reference_exchange_rates/html/index.en.html), specifically its **Latest reference rates → XML** link, which resolved to the [daily XML feed](https://www.ecb.europa.eu/stats/eurofxref/eurofxref-daily.xml). I fetched that public feed **once** for schema inspection on 2026-09-24. Its `gesmes:Envelope` contained one dated `Cube time='2026-09-24'` and one `Cube currency='USD' rate='1.1367'`; these are source/schema observations, **not** valuation inputs or test fixtures. The ECB page identifies EUR as base, says updates are usually around 16:00 CET on working days other than TARGET closing days, and says the rates are informational and strongly discourages transaction execution. The feed carries a **date, not an intraday publication timestamp**. The XML response required no paid key; no further feed or history request was made.

The [ECB usage terms](https://www.ecb.europa.eu/services/using-our-site/disclaimer/html/index.en.html) allow free use of directly obtained information subject to accurate reproduction, ECB attribution, and explicit disclosure of modification; they add a notice requirement when information is incorporated in documents that are sold. Show “Indicative EUR equivalent · ECB rate dated YYYY-MM-DD” (or USD equivalent), link to the source, and state in optional details that Mica calculated an equivalent from the ECB rate. Do not use the ECB name/logo as an endorsement or call the result an executable exchange price. Before a paid document or subscription incorporates this information, review the terms' buyer-notice requirement. This source check does not settle rights to provider sale data or future commercial distribution.

**Recommendation:** ECB daily XML supports the intended *informational current equivalent* with attribution. It does not support intraday trading prices, historical transaction conversion, or a claim that a weekend/holiday quote is live. No source blocker was found for the proposed display slice. Production activation still needs the roadmap's **separate conversion approval**.

## Existing path and proposed boundary

- `app.js` `valuationContextForItem`, `detailContextKey`, `detailValuationMarkup` and `bindDetailValuationControls` use `context.currency` as the **native evidence currency**. The `#detailValuationCurrency` selector changes that context, clears detail sales/pricing and requests new evidence. It is not a display-currency switch. `loadSales`/`fetchSaleEvidence` request `/api/sales`, preserve prior evidence on failure, and reject delayed responses unless owner, session version, selected item and context still match.
- `lib/pricing.js` `exactSoldValuation` requires a validated exact context and currency, excludes other currencies, returns `estimate: null` for unresolved/insufficient evidence and retains evidence IDs, evaluation time, sale freshness, confidence and exclusions. `app.js` `renderExactSoldValue` shows the native amount, native currency, count, confidence and expandable source evidence. `lib/core.js` `money` formats an amount in its supplied currency with `Intl.NumberFormat`.
- A profile `displayCurrency` field exists in `lib/supabase-data.js`, but `saveProfile` currently writes USD and the detail estimate does not use it. It is **not** a reliable existing user preference for this slice. Recommend an explicit, local “Show EUR equivalent” / “Show USD equivalent” control on the existing sold-derived estimate card. Default is **native only** on each detail entry. Do not repurpose `#detailValuationCurrency`, alter profile persistence, or issue another sales request when toggling the equivalent.
- Convert only a finite, eligible, non-null `exactSoldValuation` result in USD or EUR. Keep its native estimate first and its original currency, evidence IDs, coverage, rule version and confidence untouched. Do not convert sale rows into another pool, change the three-sale minimum, or modify grading, inventory, transaction, portfolio or history amounts. A converted USD estimate remains backed by **USD-market evidence**, not EUR-market coverage.

## Proposed rate and calculation contract

The immutable accepted record is `{sourceId:'ecb-eurofxref-daily', sourceUrl:'https://www.ecb.europa.eu/stats/eurofxref/eurofxref-daily.xml', base:'EUR', quote:'USD', units:'USD per EUR', rate:<positive finite decimal>, effectiveDate:'YYYY-MM-DD', fetchedAt:<actual UTC ISO fetch time>, contentSha256:<SHA-256 of the accepted response bytes>}`. `effectiveDate` is the ECB date; `fetchedAt` is *our observation time*. Neither is an invented ECB publication time. Validate the calendar date and every field before accepting the record. Use a stable `rateRef` made from source ID, effective date and content hash; retain it in the in-memory rendered-conversion object together with the exact native estimate/currency and estimator `evaluatedAt`/rule version. The user-facing details can show source, rate date, rate, native market and conversion direction; the hash belongs in diagnostics or reproducible tests. No database write is proposed.

For native amount `A` and rate `R` USD per EUR:

| Native → display | Calculation | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| USD → EUR | `A / R` | USD divided by USD per EUR |
| EUR → USD | `A × R` | EUR multiplied by USD per EUR |
| USD → USD or EUR → EUR | `A` | Identity; no FX fetch or FX attribution |

Check `A` finite and eligible and `R > 0` finite before calculation; reject a non-finite result. Keep the unrounded numeric native estimate and conversion in memory, format to two minor units **only at display** with the existing `money` formatter. Never feed a formatted or rounded equivalent back into the estimator. Do not replace an old rendered value with a new rate under its old date/reference: a successful refresh creates a new conversion object atomically, with its new source date and `rateRef`; while loading or on failure, preserve the native estimate and mark the equivalent unavailable. A future persisted valuation, if separately scoped, must snapshot the rate and original estimate together rather than resolve `rateRef` against a mutable latest-rate cache.

## Fetch, freshness and failure contract

Add one same-origin read-only `/api/fx` route. It fetches **only** the fixed HTTPS ECB daily XML URL on the server; accept no caller-supplied URL, currency or date. Disable redirects, require HTTP 200 and an XML content type, enforce a 5-second abort and a 16 KiB streamed byte cap (including missing/misleading `Content-Length`). Reject DTD/entities, unexpected envelope/structure, multiple dated cubes, multiple USD cubes, missing USD, malformed numeric text, zero/negative/non-finite rate, impossible date, and an effective date after the current UTC calendar date. A tiny, strict extractor for this fixed feed is feasible with the existing Node runtime and no production dependency; fail closed on an ECB format change rather than silently interpreting a different schema. Unit tests must attack duplicate attributes/cubes, reordered harmless whitespace/attributes and hostile XML constructs. Hash the validated raw bytes, never a reformatted rate. Return only the validated rate record and bounded status, not arbitrary upstream text.

Use a shared public success cache with a **one-hour maximum age** (`s-maxage=3600` in production; an in-process cache may reduce same-instance requests). Do not assume serverless memory is durable. Cache errors with `no-store`; no cron or persistent FX table. An unexpired successful cache entry may be reused with its original `fetchedAt`, rate and hash. Do not extend its age merely because it was served again. A manual “Try rate again” action uses the same route after an error; it must not trigger `/api/sales`. Browser code should use a bounded same-origin timeout and distinguish `loading`, `available`, `stale`, `unavailable` and `error` from the sales status.

**Freshness rule:** compare the ECB `effectiveDate` with the current **UTC calendar date**, not the viewer's timezone or the time of day. A valid record 0–4 calendar days old is eligible for an indicative equivalent, always labeled with its actual rate date. Five or more calendar days old is stale and suppresses the converted amount. This allows an ordinary Friday quote through Monday and a short closure through Tuesday, without inventing a Saturday/Sunday rate or claiming the quote is live. An unusually long TARGET closure or delayed publication can therefore suppress conversion conservatively; native valuation stays visible and retry is available. The one-hour fetch cache controls transport, while the four-day rule controls rate age; neither extends the other. Reevaluate age at render/time boundary even if the same record remains cached.

If a fetch fails, times out, returns malformed/duplicate/missing/future data, or produces a stale record, show the native estimate and “Equivalent unavailable” with a compact reason and retry. **Never output zero, a guessed rate, or a previously valid equivalent as a fresh one.** Keep the last validated record in memory for diagnostic provenance, but a failed refresh cannot certify current availability; successful revalidation is required before displaying an equivalent again. Sale-data staleness remains the existing estimator status and is shown separately from FX age/error; FX cannot upgrade sale evidence confidence. When the native estimate is missing or insufficient, do not fetch or show a converted numeric value.

## Existing-screen behavior

On the graded detail card's `renderExactSoldValue` section, keep the primary native estimate and its native evidence label. When a native estimate exists, add one secondary control: “Show EUR equivalent” for USD evidence or “Show USD equivalent” for EUR evidence. On request, show a small loading state, then a secondary line such as `About €80.00 · ECB rate dated 2026-09-24` and an optional detail with `1 EUR = 1.25 USD` and “Converted from USD completed-sale evidence.” The numeric example here is synthetic. A source link opens the ECB page in a full new window. The control can switch back to native-only without changing any evidence. On a missing/stale rate, replace only that secondary line with “Equivalent unavailable · rate needs refresh” and a retry button. Keep the native result and its sale-confidence meter visible. No modal, tutorial, forced setting or change to the market-hero quote is proposed.

The display intent and conversion response must be scoped to owner ID, `sessionLoadVersion`, `detailSelectionKey`, `detailContextKey`, native estimate/evaluation identity and a request generation. Item/copy, evidence-currency, grade/label, account or route changes cancel or invalidate pending FX display. A late previous response may update a shared validated public rate cache, but may **not** paint an equivalent on the new item/account, restore a turned-off toggle, or mutate the previous estimate. When the native sales estimate refreshes, recompute once from the new native result and a still valid rate snapshot (with visibly paired provenance), or remove the equivalent until a valid snapshot is ready. No owner data is sent to the ECB.
