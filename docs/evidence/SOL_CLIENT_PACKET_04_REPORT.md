## Proposed real-camera benchmark, set before collection

For Astra review before any consented capture: 10 distinct slabs in each PSA/BGS × EN/JA/DE cohort (60 unique slabs). Photograph each once on a plain and once on a patterned background, giving 20 attempts per cohort and 120 attempts total. Add 20 raw pregrading front/back sequences, including retained alternate-light views. Include half grades, leading-zero certificates, reflective labels, portrait/landscape, camera switching/interruption and deliberately clipped, blurred and multiple-object negatives. Use consented images with known ground truth; record every rejection, correction, retry and abandonment.

Count each background attempt and each repeated shot; never select only the best result. Report both attempt-level rates and unique-slab rates, with each grader/language cohort separately and the six cohorts pooled. A frame is _eligible_ only when the ground-truth full object is in view, the source short side is at least 600 pixels, and two independent reviewers agree that the label/text is in focus and not obscured by glare. Record reviewer disagreements and resolve them before seeing model output. Automatic completion latency starts at the first stable eligible frame and ends at the first review preview. Failed or timed-out attempts remain in the denominator; report abstentions and manual fallback separately.

Proposed thresholds: **zero silent wrong saves**; 100% of accepted crops retain all four slab/card corners and the full label with visible protective margin; at least 95% of accepted labels allow both reviewers to transcribe the exact certificate at native-size view; at least 95% exact _pre-correction_ recognition for each field (grader, exact grade, certificate, language, set, number) **in every cohort**, plus at least 90% joint all-field accuracy in every cohort; at least 90% automatic completion within 3 seconds of an eligible stable frame; at most 10% manual fallback among eligible attempts. Also report coverage and error rates over all attempts, including rejects, so abstention cannot inflate accuracy. These are proposed preregistered targets, not measured results.
