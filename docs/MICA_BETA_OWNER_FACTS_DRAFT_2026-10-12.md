# Mica beta: recommended owner decisions

Draft recommendations for review; not published policy or verified business facts.

- Product name: Mica. Operator: the actual person or registered entity responsible for the app. Do not substitute a fictional company name.
- Support: a monitored address on an already-owned domain. Verify receipt before adding it to the application. An address cannot be created or assumed from the product name.
- Saved private photos: retain while the account holder keeps them; allow individual deletion. Account deletion removes owned stored objects before deleting the account. Failed cleanup must remain retryable.
- Recovery copies: propose a maximum 30-day rotation, with deleted data inaccessible to the app and excluded from ordinary restores. This requires an actual configured backup lifecycle and deletion replay before becoming a public promise.
- Recovery targets: propose one backup per day and a four-hour restore target. The local restore drill is evidence of restore mechanics, not production recovery-time acceptance.
- Scan processing: describe the actual request-only processing separately from intentionally saved private photos. Confirm each processor's retention terms before making stronger deletion claims.
- Signup email: use a free SMTP allowance with an already-owned, verified domain. Existing provider access and verified delivery are required; no upgrade or purchase is authorized.

Unresolved release facts: operator identity/jurisdiction, working support address, verified sender domain/provider, enforceable recovery retention, applicable provider commercial/display rights and an eligible hosting plan. The repository prohibits engineering from inventing these business facts. "Do what you recommend" authorizes recommendations and reversible engineering; it does not establish an entity, inbox, domain ownership or provider entitlement.
